How much signal each recording survives the quality screen

Record 01 accepts only 1 of 48 windows (2.1%), and the rate derived from what survives still lands 2.52 bpm from the monitor. That is the screen doing its job: refusing to derive a rate from signal it judges corrupt, rather than averaging noise into a confident-looking number.
